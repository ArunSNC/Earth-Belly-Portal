The 6 Tastes of Balance

Ayurvedic nutrition emphasizes including all six tastes in every meal for complete satisfaction and balanced digestion.

  • Sweet: Grounding and nourishing (Grains, Milk, Sweets)
  • Sour: Cleansing and stimulating (Lemon, Yogurt, Pickles)
  • Salty: Warming and hydrating (Salt, Seaweed)
  • Pungent: Stimulating and clearing (Chili, Ginger, Garlic)
  • Bitter: Cooling and detoxifying (Green Leafy Veg, Turmeric)
  • Astringent: Cooling and firming (Beans, Pomegranate)

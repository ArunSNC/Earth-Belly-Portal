Alchemy

Cooking as Meditation

In the Ayurvedic tradition, the state of mind of the cook is just as important as the ingredients. Preparing food with love and mindfulness infuses it with Prana (vital life force).

We encourage you to turn your kitchen into a sanctuary. Slow down, notice the colors, and breathe in the aromas. This is where healing truly begins.

The Rhythm of Nature

Our menu changes with the seasons to ensure you're eating what your body needs most right now.

Spring (Vata-Kapha)

Focus on light, warming, and pungent foods to clear winter stagnation.

Summer (Pitta)

Cooling, hydrating, and sweet flavors to balance the heat.

Autumn/Winter (Vata)

Grounded, warm, and oily foods to maintain stability and warmth.
